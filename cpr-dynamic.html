How the figures were measured

  • Energy — the generator feeds the site's three 50 kW inverters, which log the generator input every 5 minutes. Their total for 13 Jul → 3 Oct is 4.95 % below the controller (9,737 kWh), so each period is scaled up by 4.95 % to match the controller's 10,219 kWh.
  • Run hours — from the same 5-minute data (minutes with the generator delivering), scaled to the controller's 87 h 47 min.
  • Diesel — every delivery is 500 L (supplier statement), 8 deliveries since installation.

What we ask Dynamic to check

At 106–138 kWe the engine has burned 42–56 L/h, where the datasheet gives 31–40 L/h.

  1. Fuel system: injectors, injection pump timing, return line, any leak.
  2. Air filter and intake / exhaust restriction.
  3. Governor and speed setting (1500 rpm, 50 Hz).
  4. Controller kWh and run-hour counters (CT ratio set correctly).

Next on our side: a sealed tank test — tank level, controller kWh and hours read before and after a week with no delivery.
