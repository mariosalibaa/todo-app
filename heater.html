The module

  • MOES MS-103 — Temperature and Humidity Smart Switch Module, 90–250 V AC, 15 A per gang (15 A total), Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz.
  • Terminals left to right: L2 and L1 are the two relay outputs (channel 2 and channel 1 above — unused here, the module only reads the probe), G earth, L and N the supply.
  • The temperature probe plugs into the jack on the left; the blue LED is Wi-Fi, the round button resets the pairing.
