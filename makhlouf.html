Panel efficiency does not enter the kWh: everything is per watt of panel (kWh/kWp), so a 645 W panel at 21–23 % makes the same kWh per watt as a 540 W one — efficiency only sets the area (≈ 2.8–3.1 m² for a 645 W panel, ≈ 1,700–1,850 m² of panels for 600). What does change kWh per watt: tilt and facing, shading, dust, heat (≈ −0.3 %/°C) and system losses (PVGIS assumes 14 %).
Switching: MTS (manual) picks G1 or G2; the ATS (automatic, InteliLite AMF25) swaps between EDL and the generator. The logger sits between the two, so it goes dark whenever the ATS is on EDL. On 4 Oct: EDL 00:32–03:15 (went off on G2, came back on G1), EDL 10:02–12:09, MTS swap G1 → G2 at 14:28 (under a minute). To confirm: EDL hours on a typical day. · AMF25 photo (30 Sept) showed L3 highest, the logger shows A — phase names may not match.
