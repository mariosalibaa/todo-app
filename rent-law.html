Rent law — non-residential leases

Three papers received 16-9-2026 · originals in Dropbox 0. SHIFT \ 0. RENT LAW إيجارات

In one paragraph

Old-rent non-residential leases (contracts signed before 23-7-1992) were freed by Law 11 of 5-6-2025. Law 24 of 14-8-2025 softens that: the old lease now runs on for 5, 6, 7 or 8 more years depending on the case, the rent climbs each year by a share of the gap between the old rent and the fair rent (5 % of the vacant sale value per year), and only then does the lease become free. The State as a tenant follows a separate 2023 circular (rent ×7 for three years, or +5 %/year), and the CAS inflation letter is the official basis whenever a rent has to be indexed.

1 · Law 24 / 2025 — amends Law 11 / 2025 (leases of non-residential premises)

Official Gazette no. 36, 21-8-2025, pp. 2474–2476 · signed Baabda 14-8-2025 by President Joseph Aoun and PM Nawaf Salam · in force on publication

Art. 1 — how long the old lease is extended (new Art. 3)

CaseExtension
1. Ordinary non-residential lease signed and whose original term ended before 23-7-19925 years
2. Lease born from a تفرغ (transfer) of a commercial establishment under Legislative Decree 11/67, transferred more than 10 years before Law 11/2025 was published6 years
3. Same, transferred within the 10 years before Law 11/20257 years
4. Premises occupied by public-law persons (State, ministries, Lebanese University, public institutions) and premises of a regulated free profession (pharmacies etc.)8 years
(b) Any non-residential premises over 500 m² where the tenant paid, with the landlord's knowledge, costs that were the landlord's (rebuilding, structural repairs, façades, common parts, added areas). Disputes → single civil judge for lease matters.8 years
  • A tenant of an establishment whose licence requires a minimum distance from a similar business and/or a minimum floor area may, once only, move to new premises with that distance and/or area cut in half.
  • (c) When the period ends the lease becomes free — subject to the will of the two parties.

Art. 2 — fair rent (new Art. 4)

  • Badal al-mithl = 5 % per year of the sale value of the premises in its present state, as if vacant (was 8 % in Law 11).

Art. 3 — how the rent rises during the extension (new Art. 6)

Each year the tenant pays the old rent + a share of the difference between the old rent and the fair rent:
Year of extensionCases 1 & 2Cases 3, 4 and (b)
1st30 %30 %
2nd40 %40 %
3rd50 %50 %
4thfull fair rent (case 1: years 4–5; case 2: years 4–6)60 %
5th → 8th—full fair rent

Art. 4 — tenant's compensation (Art. 10-d)

  • The compensation owed to the tenant on leaving shrinks in proportion to the extension already enjoyed — by 1/5, 1/6, 1/7 or 1/8 for each year elapsed, according to the case.

Art. 5 & 6

  • Everything contrary is repealed; lawsuits filed before this law stays under the law they were filed under.
  • In force from publication (21-8-2025).

Why parliament changed Law 11 (reasons stated in the Gazette)

  • Law 11 freed all non-residential leases after 4 years — too short for transferred commercial businesses (Decree 11/67) and for public bodies (Ministry of Education, Lebanese University…) that would have to relocate.
  • It lowered the fair rent from 8 % to 5 % to keep the yearly increases bearable, took away the landlord's option to waive the increases, and set the 5/6/7/8-year ladder.
  • Since 2022 no extension law had been passed, so courts were applying the Code of Obligations to these contracts — a legal vacuum the law closes.

2 · Circular 22 / 2023 — rents of buildings leased by the State

PM Najib Mikati, Beirut 11-9-2023 · to all ministries, public administrations and institutions, municipalities and unions · applies Council of Ministers decision no. 1 of 7-9-2023 (committee set up by decision 58/2023 of 8-5-2023)
  • Public administrations, institutions and municipalities under Court of Accounts control, plus Army and security-force leases under Law 159 of 22-7-1992: rent becomes 7 × the rent in force on 31-12-2022, fixed for a 3-year contract period, then re-negotiated.
  • Leases governed by Law 160 of 22-7-1992 (built property): the increase in Art. 38 as amended by Law 2 of 28-3-2017 — 5 % per year.
  • Excluded: leases signed in US dollars after 31-12-2022 that already reflected market prices; those are referred to the competent authority.
